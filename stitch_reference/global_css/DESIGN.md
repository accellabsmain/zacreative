## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-end editorial presence tailored for next-generation digital intelligence, design engineering, and creative technology studios. The interface balances high-fashion publishing poise with technical precision: generous architectural whitespace punctuated by dark obsidian modular monoliths and restrained, luminous electric ambient flares.

### Emotional Response & Aesthetic Philosophy
- **Authoritative & Quietly Confident:** The interface speaks with typographic discipline rather than noise. Generous baseline rhythmic whitespace evokes luxury art journals and modernist institutional monographs.
- **Architectural & Crystalline:** Strict 1px hairline rules, disciplined grid columns, and hairline data dividers structure content like technical blueprints.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Controlled cyan and royal blue atmospheric mesh blurs illuminate behind obsidian containers, providing an aura of advanced computing and vitality without descending into ungrounded novelty.

### Design Movement Synthesis
A curated synthesis of **Contemporary Editorial Modernism** and **Atmospheric Glass Tech**. Layouts follow strict structural grids with generous column gutters, large-scale medium-weight headlines with relaxed tracking, crisp monospaced or micro-scaled data callouts, and modular dark-mode obsidian blocks that anchor the luminous canvas.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast daylight clarity set against deep celestial voids. The pure white ground provides an airy editorial canvas, while deep obsidian navy introduces technical weight and focused grounding.

### Primary Roles & Accents
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure, uncompromised white canvas for unmatched editorial clarity and high contrast.
- **Obsidian Dark Void (`#0A1120` & `#103B75`):** Used for focal modular pods, high-impact banners, structural toolbars, and inverted feature showcases.
- **Brand Royal Blue (`#1A54A4`):** Primary semantic interactive anchor; drives primary action pathways, active indicators, and high-priority states.
- **Electric Cyan Glow (`#00D2FF` & `#38BDF8`):** Restrained to ambient luminescence meshes, active status beacons, and interactive micro-highlights. Never used for large text blocks on light surfaces.
- **Atmospheric Tints (`#EBF2FA` & `#E0EDFB`):** Soft, structural tints for secondary containers, badge backings, and subtle table highlight rows.
- **Hairline Rule (`#E2E8F0` / `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`):** 1px structural framing across borders, column split lines, and modular dividers.

### Color Coupling Guidelines
Always pair `#00D2FF` ambient glows behind dark obsidian (`#0A1120`) surfaces or layered glass modules. Keep text contrast exceeding WCAG AAA standards: text on pure white uses `#0A1120` or `#334155`; text on obsidian cards utilizes pure white `#FFFFFF` or softened silver `#94A3B8`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a dialogue between editorial gravitas and analytical engineering.

### Type Pairings & Behavioral Directives
- **Headlines & Metric Figures (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Configured in medium weights (500) rather than standard heavy bold styles. Tight negative tracking creates sculpted, editorial titles that mirror Swiss architectural publications.
- **Editorial & Interface Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, optically balanced, providing high legibility across dense project specs, body copy, and UI controls.
- **Technical Micro-Labels & Badges:** Rendered in `Inter` uppercase with deliberate wide tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) at 11px and 12px. These act as crisp technical timestamps, categorical tags, and metadata points.
- **Metric Numerals (`data-metric`):** Stripped of heavy weight, rendered large and architectural, providing clean data-driven confidence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a mathematically disciplined 12-column grid across desktop environments, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **Rhythmic Vertical Lines:** Editorial structure is achieved using 1px vertical hairline borders along grid gutters (`#E2E8F0`). Content modules often dock directly against these lines, creating architectural columns.
- **Section Margins:** Desktop section pads rely on expansive vertical pacing (`space-xl` scaled to multiple multiples such as 5rem to 8rem) to allow editorial headlines room to breathe.
- **Asymmetric Split Blocks:** Key responsive layouts leverage 50/50 or 60/40 splits where a pristine white canvas module sits flush beside a deep obsidian-navy modular card.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Split columns collapse into clean stacked slabs. Hairline borders switch from vertical gutters to horizontal row separators to preserve the structural blueprint aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system bypasses heavy, muddy drop-shadows. Instead, elevation is expressed through **luminous atmospheric back-glows**, **translucent obsidian glass**, and **crisp hairline boundaries**.

### The Ambient Mesh Aura
- **Light Emitting Sources:** Behind obsidian cards or hero anchors, generate radial blur meshes utilizing `#00D2FF` and `#1A54A4` with blur radii between `80px` and `120px` at `0.15` to `0.28` opacity.
- **Surface Containment:** The glow must remain diffuse and soft, creating an illuminated aura that peeks around card perimeters rather than flooding foreground content.

### Surface Tiers & Hairline Edges
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure solid `#FFFFFF`. Zero elevation, delineated by `#E2E8F0` hairline rules.
- **Soft Tint Surface (Level 1):** `#EBF2FA` or `#F8FAFC` for secondary data matrices, with 1px hairline border and zero blur shadow.
- **Obsidian Monolith (Level 2):** Solid `#0A1120` or translucent `rgba(10, 17, 32, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Controls (Level 3):** Modal sheets and navigation pills use `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(10, 17, 32, 0.08)` coupled with a distinct 1px hairline border.

## Shapes

The geometric signature balances architectural discipline with soft touchpoints. Primary surfaces and modular blocks use crisp, modern corners, while interaction badges and chips take on sleek capsule pills.

### Geometry Standards
- **Containers & Split Cards:** Standard container radius adheres to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for prominent monolithic blocks.
- **Buttons & Interactive Badges:** Primary and secondary actions, along with category chips, utilize full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish immediate tactile contrast against the orthogonal grid.
- **Form Controls:** Text inputs, dropdowns, and data filters use a refined `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) with strict 1px framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), background in `#1A54A4`, text in `#FFFFFF` with medium weight. Hover shifts to `#103B75` accompanied by a faint `0 0 20px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.35)` outer atmospheric glow.
- **Obsidian Dark Pill:** Deep `#0A1120` background, pure white text, framed by a 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border.
- **Ghost Editorial Button:** Transparent background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, text in `#0A1120`. On hover, fills with `#EBF2FA` and shifts border to `#CBD5E1`.

### Chips & Badges
- Capsule pills (`rounded-full`) scaled to 24px–28px in height.
- Micro uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- Light variant: `#EBF2FA` fill, `#1A54A4` text, optional 1px border in `rgba(26, 84, 164, 0.12)`.
- Obsidian Glow variant: `rgba(10, 17, 32, 0.8)` fill, `#00D2FF` text, 1px border in `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`, with a 4px pulsating live cyan status dot.

### Cards & Split Modular Blocks
- **Canvas White Card:** White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border, no shadow. Content separated by horizontal or vertical hairline dividers.
- **Obsidian Glow Card:** `#0A1120` solid core, `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.12)`. Positioned over an absolute ambient radial blur (`80px-120px` cyan/navy mesh) at the bottom or top corner. Content uses pure white titles and `#94A3B8` body copy.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height: 44px on desktop, 48px on mobile.
- Clean white or `#F8FAFC` background framed by 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border.
- Typography set to `body-md` in `#0A1120`. Placeholder styled in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1A54A4` with a sharp `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.15)` focus ring.

### Data Metric Displays
- Compact metric blocks featuring a large `data-metric` figure above or beside a `label-sm` uppercase caption.
- Separated by vertical 1px `#E2E8F0` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` divider rules.
- Optional trend delta chip (e.g., `+18.4%`) embedded in a pill badge with high-contrast indicator arrows.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (`rounded` 4px for checkboxes) or circle (for radios).
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Checked: `#1A54A4` fill with pure white glyph, surrounded by a subtle `0 0 8px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.4)` aura.
